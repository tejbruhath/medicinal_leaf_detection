Tulsi

Ocimum tenuiflorum

Adaptogen Anti-inflammatory Antioxidant

Medicinal Profile

Traditional Uses

Sacred in Ayurveda for over 3,000 years. Used to treat respiratory ailments, fever, and digestive disorders. Revered as an adaptogen to help the body manage stress. Commonly brewed as herbal tea for immunity and mental clarity.

Side Effects

May thin the blood — avoid prior to surgery. High doses can lower blood sugar; diabetics should monitor closely. Pregnant women should consult a physician before consumption. Rare allergic reactions reported in sensitive individuals.

Home Remedies

Cold & cough: Boil 8–10 leaves in water with ginger and honey, drink warm.

Headache: Apply a paste of crushed leaves to the forehead.

Skin clarity: Use leaf-infused water as a daily face rinse.
