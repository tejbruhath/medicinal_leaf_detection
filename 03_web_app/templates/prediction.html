Aloe Vera: Medical Uses and Treatments

Aloe Vera is a versatile plant that has been used for centuries due to its medicinal properties. It contains compounds that are beneficial for skin health, digestion, and boosting the immune system.

Medical Uses

  • Digestive Health: Improves digestion and relieves constipation.

  • Immunity: Antioxidants strengthen the immune system.
  • Diabetes: May help lower blood sugar levels.
